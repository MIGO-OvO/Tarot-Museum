# Impeccable Design Guidelines

## Design Context

### Users
The primary audience is **serious tarot students** — readers who study symbolism, numerology (毕达哥拉斯灵数), astrology, alchemy, and the Kabbalistic Tree of Life in depth. They arrive in a focused, study-minded state and stay to read long-form scholarly content in Chinese (zh-CN), with English and Hebrew terms woven throughout. The job to be done: understand each card's hidden meaning across multiple interpretive dimensions, treating the site as an authoritative reference manuscript rather than a quick-lookup tool.

This is **阿卡纳手稿馆 · THE ARCANUM GALLERY** — a static-HTML scholarly exhibition of all 78 tarot cards (22 Major + 56 Minor Arcana), presented as a curated "manuscript hall" with a gallery index and per-card deep-dive pages.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **scholarly, reverent, archival.**

Voice and tone: the measured, knowing voice of a museum curator or a manuscript annotator — confident in its expertise, never breathless or salesy. Bilingual by nature (Chinese prose with Latin/Hebrew esoteric terms), it treats the reader as a fellow student of the mysteries.

Emotional goal: **reverent and contemplative.** The interface should feel museum-hushed — a space that invites the reader to slow down, dwell, and absorb. Quiet authority over spectacle.

### Aesthetic Direction
An **antique illuminated-manuscript / fine-press exhibition catalogue** rendered for the web. The established visual language:

- **Palette (light only):** warm aged-paper grounds in `oklch` (`--bg` ~95% L, `--paper` ~97% L, warm hue ~85), deep warm-ink foreground (`--fg` ~24% L), with two precious accents — **gold** (`--gold` / `--gold-deep`, hue ~70) for ornament and emphasis, and **wine red** (`--wine` / `--wine-soft`, hue ~25) for active/hover states and key terms. Subtle dotted-grid paper texture via layered radial gradients.
- **Typography:** serif body (`Iowan Old Style` / `Palatino` / `Songti SC` / `Source Han Serif SC`), a dedicated Song-style face (`--song`) for Chinese display moments, monospace (`--mono`) reserved for eyebrows, metadata, plate numbers, and English labels (uppercase, wide letter-spacing), and a system sans (`--sans`) for utilitarian chrome like tabs and tags.
- **Ornament & detail:** gold hairline rules with transparent gradient fades, glyph dividers (`✦ ❖ ✦`), inset gold "frame" borders on cards, generous letter-spacing on small caps. Detail pages use a sticky blurred topbar and a refined section rhythm.
- **Motion:** restrained scroll-reveal (fade + 30px rise on `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.46,0.45,0.94)`), gentle card lift and image scale on hover, a single hero entrance animation. Motion should always feel like turning a page, never like an app UI.
- **Theme:** **light only.** The warm-paper palette is the single intentional theme; do not introduce a dark mode.

**Anti-references — what this must NOT become:**
- **Not New-Age kitsch** — no purple-galaxy gradients, neon mysticism, glitter, or clip-art crystals and moons.
- **Not generic SaaS / AI** — no flat cards, Inter-everywhere, rounded-everything, default startup landing-page look.
- **Not loud or commercial** — no hard-sell CTAs, popups, exclamation energy, or marketplace vibes.
- **Not sterile / clinical** — never strip away the warmth, ornament, and paper texture in the name of minimalism.

### Design Principles
1. **Manuscript, not app.** Every element should read as part of a printed exhibition catalogue — page rhythm, plate numbers, hairline rules — not as web UI chrome. When a choice is ambiguous, pick the one a fine-press book would make.
2. **Quiet authority.** Earn trust through restraint, accuracy, and craft. No persuasion tactics; the content's depth is the draw. Whitespace and stillness are features.
3. **Two precious accents, used sparingly.** Gold for ornament and reverence, wine for emphasis and interaction. Their power comes from scarcity against warm paper — never flood the page with color.
4. **Bilingual hierarchy with intent.** Serif Chinese carries the prose; mono Latin/Hebrew carries labels and esoterica. Respect both scripts' legibility and the wide-letterspaced label convention already in place.
5. **Motion that turns the page.** Animation is slow, eased, and purposeful — revealing content as the reader descends. If an effect calls attention to itself, it's wrong.
6. **Reverence in the details.** Inset frames, ornament glyphs, considered spacing, and accessible contrast are the difference between decoration and devotion. Maintain WCAG-conscious contrast even within the warm low-chroma palette.
